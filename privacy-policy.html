Privacy policy

Last update: 1st Feb 2021

Who we are

Our website address is: https://heidipun.com. This website is run by Heidi Pungartnik, s.p., Celovška cesta 136, Ljubljana, 1000 Ljubljana.

What information do we collect?

We collect information when you visit the website with Google Analytics and other types of tracking. You may, however, visit our site anonymously by blocking cookies. We collect your information and message if you submit the contact form.

We may collect data with:
Google Analytics (Which pages are visited, where the traffic comes from, etc. We use this to improve user experience. The data is anonymised.)
Hotjar (Responses in polls, mouse tracking, movement through the site, IP address. We use this to improve user experience. The data is anonymised.)
Formcarry (If you submit the contact form. This data is not anonymised.)
We retain these data indefinitely.

You may ask for a copy of your data or its removal at any time. This only applies to personally-identifiable data, namely the IP address, email address, name, etc.

Do we use cookies?

Yes (Cookies are small files that a site or its service provider transfers to your computers hard drive through your Web browser (if you allow) that enables the sites or service providers systems to recognize your browser and capture and remember certain information.

We use cookies to compile aggregate data about site traffic and site interaction so that we can offer better site experiences and tools in the future.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer to outside parties your personally identifiable information. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential. We may also release your information when we believe release is appropriate to comply with the law, enforce our site policies, or protect ours or others rights, property, or safety.
Our data partners are:
  • Google (to track traffic)
  • Formcarry (if you submit the contact form)
  • Hotjar (to help track traffic and clicks)

California Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance

Because we value your privacy we have taken the necessary precautions to be in compliance with the California Online Privacy Protection Act. We therefore will not distribute your personal information to outside parties without your consent.

Childrens Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance

We are in compliance with the requirements of COPPA (Childrens Online Privacy Protection Act), we do not collect any information from anyone under 13 years of age. Our website, products and services are all directed to people who are at least 13 years old or older.

Online Privacy Policy Only

This online privacy policy applies only to information collected through our website and not to information collected offline.

Your Consent

By using our site, you consent to our privacy policy.

Changes to our Privacy Policy

If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on this page.
